For more than 20 years, Harlequin Dachshunds has raised extraordinary, healthy and happy miniature longhaired dachshund puppies in our South Jersey home. Every puppy comes from AKC-registered, titled parents and is socialized with our family from the very first day. Browse the puppies below to see the quality, color and temperament our program is known for.

The puppies shown on this page are NOT available. They are featured as examples of the puppies that we have bred, and will breed, in future litters.

We breed with careful consideration and dedication, choosing parents who will produce the best quality miniature long-haired dachshund puppies in structure and temperament. As a result, we breed a limited number of litters each year, so that we can spend time with each puppy for socialization and training. If you’re interested in owning and loving one of our puppies, please send e-mail to DoxyLadyNJ@gmail.com. Please indicate your name and where you live. We do not ship puppies anywhere, so you must personally pick up the puppy. All puppies are sold with health guarantee and on a spay/neuter contract. Puppies are reserved with a deposit only.
