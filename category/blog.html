Practical advice from Karen R. Scheiner of Harlequin Dachshunds, who has bred, trained and shown miniature longhaired dachshunds for more than 20 years, earning titles in conformation, agility and obedience. Karen is an AKC Breeder of Merit and an AKC Dachshund judge, and her dogs include MACH agility champions and a Utility Dog (UD) obedience titleholder.
