About Harlequin Dachshunds

We have been breeding beautiful, healthy AKC miniature longhaired dachshunds, exclusively, for over 20 years. We strive to produce the best quality doxies in  conformation, intelligence, and  temperament. Our dogs are outgoing and eager to please, as they have been socialized in a home environment from the earliest days of their lives. Visit the following pages to see for yourself how our dogs have excelled not only in AKC Conformation events, but in performance competitions including Agility and Obedience, as well.

The purpose of this website is not only to showcase our puppies and adult dogs, but to provide an education to those of you who are unfamiliar with the dachshund, as to the versatility of this breed.  Dachshunds can be exceptional house pets but, as they are intelligent and love to learn, they can successfully compete in a range of performance activities.  On these pages, we have shown our Miniature Longhaired Dachshunds, many of whom have ranked in the TOP TEN DACHSHUNDS in the country in CONFORMATION, AGILITY and OBEDIENCE.  You are also invited to read the BLOGS on the right of the screen for more information about training and possible activities for you and your dachshund. I hope you enjoy these pages. If you do review our blogs, please post your comments about your own experiences!!!
