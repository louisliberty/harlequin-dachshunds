About Harlequin Dachshunds

We have been breeding beautiful, healthy AKC miniature longhaired dachshunds, exclusively, for over 20 years. We strive to produce the best quality doxies in conformation, intelligence, and temperament. Our dogs are outgoing and eager to please, as they have been socialized in a home environment from the earliest days of their lives. Visit the following pages to see for yourself how our dogs have excelled not only in AKC Conformation events, but in performance competitions including Agility and Obedience, as well.

The purpose of this website is not only to showcase our puppies and adult dogs, but to provide educational information on dachshunds and the versatility of this breed. Dachshunds can be exceptional house pets but, as they’re intelligent and love to learn, they can successfully compete in a range of performance activities. On these pages, we showcase our Miniature Longhaired Dachshunds, many of whom have ranked in the top ten dachshunds in the country in conformation, agility, and obedience. Read our Blog for more information on training and activities for you and your dachshund.
