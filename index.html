About Harlequin Dachshunds

Meet Karen

Harlequin Dachshunds is where Karen R. Scheiner shares what she has learned from more than 20 years with miniature longhaired dachshunds: practical training advice on our Blog, and the records of dogs who ranked among the top ten dachshunds in the country in conformation, agility, and obedience.

More about Karen →

For over 20 years, we bred beautiful, healthy AKC miniature longhaired dachshunds, exclusively. We strove to produce the best quality doxies in conformation, intelligence, and temperament. Our dogs were outgoing and eager to please, as they were socialized in a home environment from the earliest days of their lives, and they excelled not only in AKC Conformation events, but in performance competitions including Agility and Obedience, as well.

The purpose of this website is to provide educational information on dachshunds and the versatility of this breed. Dachshunds can be exceptional house pets but, as they’re intelligent and love to learn, they can successfully compete in a range of performance activities. Visit our Obedience, Conformation and Agility pages to see for yourself how our dogs excelled.
