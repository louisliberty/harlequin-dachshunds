More formally, “CHAMPION HARLEQUIN DIAMOND IN THE RUFF ML”, Rolex earned his AKC BREED CHAMPIONSHIP on October 1, 2015  at the prestigious Morris & Essex Kennel Club Show at Somerset, NJ.  He is now working on finishing his Grand Championship. Keep posted!

Rolex was sired by “BLUE” and is much like his father. His dam is “EMERALD.” Both parents are AKC Grand Champions.  Beautiful overall silver dapple with correct conformation and an outgoing loving personality!  Photos of parents are shown below.
