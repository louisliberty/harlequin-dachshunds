More formally, “HARLEQUIN TURBO JET BLUE ML”, Jet has just begun to earn championship points as we only recently started to show him. Pictured below at Dachshund Club of America-  2016 – National Specialty, at age 2 years, winning his class!   He’s available at stud to approved bitches. Black and tan, carries chocolate.
Jet was sired by “BLUE” and is very much like his dam, “EMERALD.”  Beautiful overall black and tan with correct conformation and a winning personality!  Photos of parents are shown below.
