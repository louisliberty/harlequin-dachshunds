“Emerald” is structurally very correct, with a perfect top-line and conformation. She also has a winning personality and is eager to please. Emerald has given us some very beautiful puppies.  Look carefully at this photo, as it is a favorite. Did you notice that none of Emerald’s feet are touching the ground?!!    It’s called “suspended gaiting.”  Just incredible!
