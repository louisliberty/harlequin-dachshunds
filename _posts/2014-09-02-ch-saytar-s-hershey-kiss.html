Chocolate can be, oh so sweet! Meet Hershey, a gorgeous chocolate and tan miniature longhair. Proudly, he took “Winners Dog” at back-to-back Specialty Show Majors at the Central New Jersey Hound Association show and the Dachshund Club of New Jersey Show at Somerset Hills, in September, 2005. He made a “clean sweep” that weekend, taking Winners Dog at the Westchester Kennel Club show in Tarrytown, NY. See wins.
