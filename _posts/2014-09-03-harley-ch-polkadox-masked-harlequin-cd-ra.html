Harley is a beautiful dog with a gorgeous head, a level topline and a warm personality. He finished his AKC CHAMPIONSHIP easily and had the honor of being shown at the prestigious Westminster Kennel Club Show at Madison Square Garden in 2002 and again in 2005.  At age 16 years, Harley is now retired from the show world and enjoys resting in the sunlight or running through the weave poles!  He is the proud sire of some of our beautiful show dogs.
