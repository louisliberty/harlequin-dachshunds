We are very proud that Axyl finished her Championship easily, in only a few shows.   At just one year old, she won back to back 4- point majors in Virginia at the Hound Specialty Shows.  She also won a placement in a large, very competitive class at the Dachshund Club of America 2016 National Specialty Show!  Very proud that I bred and own this awesome little girl. Axyl has already produced  several  beautiful puppies.  We are looking forward to another extraordinary litter from her in 2018.
