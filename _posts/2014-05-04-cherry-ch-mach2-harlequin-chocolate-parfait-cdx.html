CHERRY EARNED HER MACH IN FEBRUARY, 2011 IN NEW JERSEY. CHERRY’S MACH2 WAS EARNED IN APRIL, 2012, AT THE DACHSHUND NATIONAL SPECIALTY SHOW IN GEORGIA. SHE WAS THE FIRST MINIATURE LONGHAIRED DACHSHUND IN THE COUNTRY TO EARN BOTH A BREED CHAMPIONSHIP AND A MACH! CHERRY WAS RANKED THE NUMBER ONE MINIATURE DACHSHUND IN THE COUNTRY IN 2011 AND AGAIN IN 2012; SHE PLACED IN THE TOP 5 MINIATURE DACHSHUNDS IN 2013.
