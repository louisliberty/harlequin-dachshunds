Cessna finished his Championship at Dachshund Club of America Regional Specialty show, during Memorial Day Weekend, 2018 at just over one year of age. Frankly, we’re not surprised that he finished so quickly, as he is grandson of our CH. Harlequin Morning Glory ML and CH. Old Hanover’s Phildelphian ML (both parents’ photos are on this website page.)  Cessna has beautiful silky coat, and has the rare distinction of being chocolate and tan.  Awesome conformation  with great personality!
